Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026. This version applies to Riply 1.3.0 and later.

This Cookie Policy explains how Riply TCG, operated by Scoutli Teknoloji ve Yazılım AŞ, stores information on your device. Read it together with our Privacy Policy.

1. The App

The Riply mobile app does not use browser cookies. It does use similar technologies, which keep small pieces of data in your device's storage:

What is stored Provider Why
Sign-in session Supabase Keeps you signed in
App settings, such as onboarding status and your last used collection Riply Remembers your choices
Records of events already reported, such as your first scan and logged purchases Riply Avoids counting the same event twice
Device and session identifiers Phase Product analytics
Purchaser identifier and subscription status RevenueCat Manages your subscription
Anonymous identifier and event queue Meta Advertising measurement

If you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, the Meta SDK can also read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

2. Your Choices

  • Signing out removes your sign-in session.
  • Deleting the App removes the data kept in its storage.
  • Turning off tracking in iOS Settings stops the App from reading your advertising identifier.

3. This Website

These legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not set cookies on them or use analytics. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
