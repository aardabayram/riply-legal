Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026. This version applies to Riply 1.3.0 and later.

This Privacy Policy explains how Riply TCG ("Riply", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Riply mobile application (the "App"). By using Riply, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Riply TCG is operated by Scoutli Teknoloji ve Yazılım AŞ ("the Company"), based in Türkiye. Riply is a trading card collection tracker and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Wizards of the Coast, Ravensburger, Bandai, Riot Games, or any other rights holder. All trading card game names, logos, and card images are the property of their respective owners.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. Riply uses passwordless sign-in. When you create an account we collect your email address and a username. We send a one-time login code (OTP) to your email; we do not create, store, or have access to any password.

Collection data. The cards you add to your vault, including game type, card identifiers, condition, quantity, and any optional purchase price you enter. We also store your price alerts and derived data such as daily portfolio value snapshots.

Preferences. Your preferred trading card games, your answers to onboarding questions, display settings, and notification preferences.

Card scan images. When you use the camera scanner, the photo you capture is sent — through our secure backend — to our card-identification provider, Scrydex, to recognize the card. Scan images are used only to identify the card, are not used to build a profile of you, and are not retained by us after identification.

Subscription information. If you purchase Riply Pro, your purchase is processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status and purchase details such as the plan, price, and transaction identifier. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Notification token. If you turn on notifications, we store a push token for your device with your account so we can send you price alerts.

Analytics data. We use Phase to understand how the App is used. It collects a device identifier generated by the App, your device model, operating system and app version, language and time zone, an approximate location derived from your IP address, the screens you view, and actions such as scans, cards added, and purchases. When you are signed in, this data is linked to your account identifier.

Advertising and measurement data. We share certain data with Meta to measure our advertising. Section 3 explains what is shared and the choice you have.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality;
  • Display your card collection and portfolio value;
  • Fetch real-time pricing and card data from third-party providers;
  • Identify cards you scan with the camera;
  • Send notifications you have opted into (such as price alerts);
  • Process and manage subscriptions;
  • Understand how the App is used, diagnose problems, and improve the App experience;
  • Measure which of our ads lead to installs and subscriptions, and advertise Riply.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do share limited data with Meta for advertising, as described in section 3. Some US state privacy laws may treat this as "sharing" personal information or as "targeted advertising".

3. Advertising, Tracking, and Your Choice

Riply includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK so we can measure our advertising on Meta's platforms.

What the App sends to Meta:

  • App installs and app opens;
  • Completed sign-ups and your first successful card scan;
  • Subscription starts, including the plan, price, currency, and transaction identifier;
  • Device information such as device model, operating system and app version, language, time zone, mobile carrier, and screen size;
  • An anonymous identifier created by the SDK, and the ad campaign identifier if you opened the App from an ad;
  • Crash data from the SDK.

What the App does not send to Meta: your email address, username, card collection, or scan images.

Your tracking choice. On iOS, the App uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt to ask for your permission the first time you open it.

  • If you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is also sent, and Meta may link this data with data from other companies' apps and websites to measure and target ads.
  • If you do not allow tracking, the App does not access your advertising identifier. It still sends the events above to Meta, without that identifier and marked as not permitted for tracking. Apple's SKAdNetwork may also report ad results in aggregate.

You can change your choice at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Refusing tracking does not limit any feature of the App.

Meta handles this data under the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Riply relies on the following providers:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend hosting. Your data is stored with row-level security so you can only access your own data.
  • Scrydex — card identification (camera scan) and trading card pricing/catalog data.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management.
  • Phase — product analytics, as described in section 1.
  • Meta Platforms — advertising measurement, as described in section 3.
  • Apple — In-App Purchases and (if enabled) push notifications via the Apple Push Notification service.
  • Expo — application framework, build tooling, and delivery of push notifications.

Each provider maintains its own privacy policy governing its handling of data.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure protected by row-level security policies. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because Riply uses one-time email codes, there are no passwords to be lost or stolen.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen. Deleting your account permanently removes your collection, collections, price alerts, notification tokens, preferences, portfolio history, and the account itself from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not automatically erase data already held by Meta, Phase, or RevenueCat, which they keep under their own policies. Contact us if you need help with a request to them. You may also export your collection data to CSV at any time from the Profile screen.

7. Camera & Photos

The App requests camera access solely to scan and identify your trading cards. Photo library access, if granted, is used only to let you add card images. Riply does not access your camera or photos for any other purpose, and does not upload your photo library.

8. Notifications

If you opt in, Riply may send push notifications such as price alerts and portfolio summaries. You can turn notifications on or off at any time from the Profile screen or in your device settings.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App does not use browser cookies, but it does store identifiers and settings on your device. Our Cookie Policy explains them.

10. Children's Privacy

Riply is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Delete your account and all associated data;
  • Export your collection data;
  • Withdraw your tracking permission, as described in section 3;
  • Opt out of "sharing" for advertising or of targeted advertising;
  • Opt out of non-essential notifications.

Residents of Türkiye (KVKK — Law No. 6698), the EEA/UK (GDPR), and California and other US states with privacy laws (including CCPA/CPRA) may have additional rights, including the right to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
